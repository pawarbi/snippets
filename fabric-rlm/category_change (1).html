Revenue shifts by product category: 2017-Q2 to 2018-Q2

Executive summary

This section summarises the headline movements in revenue between 2017-Q2 and 2018-Q2. The largest shift came from watches_gifts, which added BRL 232,342 in revenue (240.9%), while total revenue across all categories increased by BRL 1,830,581 (121.9%). Understanding these top-line changes helps frame where to focus deeper analysis.

Where it came from

The chart below shows the five categories with the biggest revenue increases and the five with the biggest decreases, with a zero line in the centre and bars extending left or right. It highlights that watches_gifts delivered by far the largest uplift in absolute terms, while some segments contracted even as overall revenue more than doubled. This view helps separate broad market growth from category-specific wins and losses.

Mix shift

The next view focuses on mix rather than just volume, plotting each of the eight largest categories by their share of total revenue in 2017-Q2 and 2018-Q2. It shows how fast-growing categories such as watches_gifts gained weight in the overall portfolio while others either held steady or lost ground. These mix changes matter because they influence margin, risk exposure, and where future growth is likely to come from.

Insights

This section lists concise findings drawn directly from the charts and underlying quarterly totals, with each point anchored on specific numbers rather than speculation about causes. Together, they highlight where revenue is expanding, where it is shrinking, and how the category mix is evolving over time.

  • In 2018-Q2, watches_gifts revenue rose from BRL 96,449 to BRL 328,790, an increase of BRL 232,342 (240.9%), and its share of quarterly revenue moved from 6.4% to 9.9%.
  • Total revenue grew from BRL 1,501,576 in 2017-Q2 to BRL 3,332,157 in 2018-Q2, a gain of BRL 1,830,581 (121.9%), showing a broad uplift in demand.
  • Growth was broad-based: 59 categories increased while 14 declined, indicating that the expansion spanned far more segments than it left behind.
  • The steepest decline was in Uncategorised, which fell from BRL 29,401 to BRL 16,451, a drop of BRL 12,950 and a share shift from 2.0% to 0.5%.

How this was checked

All figures use the [Total Revenue] measure from the Sales table, defined as SUM(Sales[Line Total]), aggregated at the product category and quarter level. The analysis compares the latest complete quarter in the dataset, 2018-Q2, with the same quarter a year earlier, 2017-Q2; later data (2018-Q3) is marked as partial in the model's coverage report and was therefore excluded. Totals for each quarter are based on Total Revenue across all products, including an explicit Uncategorised group for items without a category, so the sum of category revenues matches the full-quarter total used in this report.
